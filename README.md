During my course of SE461, Software Testing and Quality Assurance, we were tasked to test the entire game of tetris on the IDE eclipse in the programming language Java. Hoping to get as close to 100% test coverage, my team got to a jacoco score of 80.6%. I was looking back on some of the work that I did during my college course to update on my github, and I had saved this bad boy on my local device for a couple years. 

Our professor even tasked us with locating missing errors within the game too! We managed to find them whenever running the actual game or looking into all the modules. Like the one shown above when the score updated to a very high number! It was our job to search and make note of them as we tested everything. Looking back it now with the experience I have, it was very easy to install and get a feel as to what I was looking at! It's very cool to know I continued to grow as a software engineer. 

Despite it being an assignment, I really do love games and this was the first ever game that I was ablel to try and test and made sure everything worked correctly. We even managed to import a little robot package that would play the game each time we decided to run our test suite! The purpose of importing this litte guy into our test suite was to play the entire game, meaning it would do it's best to cover every single area of our game. Turn off test half way and your jacoco score would be incomplete. Playing through the entire thing, resulted in an accurate score.

It can be viewed if you decide to install the zip file on your local device! Used JUnit 4 to do all the testing because it was more stable. Also, keeping it here as a reminder to always document my experience and previous work.
